You can delete your PrankFX account and everything in it yourself, from inside the app. No form, no waiting for us to approve it.

Delete in the app

  1. Open PrankFX and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, then tap it once more to confirm.

The deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Deletion by email

If you cannot sign in, write to maxim.maslii777@gmail.com from the email address of the account, with the subject DELETE MY ACCOUNT. We verify that the request comes from the account owner and complete the deletion within 30 days — normally within a few days.

What is deleted

  • Your account record: email, name, profile picture URL, password hash, sign-in method, creation date.
  • Your photo history: every original and every generated result stored on our server.
  • Your Snap clips: the finished videos, their thumbnails and the job records, including the files on our server's disk.
  • Your FX credit balance and subscription status. Unused credits are forfeited — spend them before deleting if you want to use them.

The raw video you record for a Snap clip is not part of this: it is deleted from the server as soon as the clip has been assembled, whether or not you delete your account.

What may remain, and for how long

  • Server logs (IP address, timestamps, endpoints) — up to 30 days, then removed automatically.
  • Encrypted backups — overwritten on their normal rotation, no later than 30 days after deletion.
  • Purchase records — kept where tax and accounting law requires it. These records show that a transaction happened; they are not your content.

Things we do not control

Results you already saved to your phone's gallery or shared elsewhere stay where you put them — delete those yourself. Purchases made through Google Play or the App Store are recorded in your store account; refunds and receipts are handled by the store. Signing in with Google does not give us access to your Google account, and deleting your PrankFX account does not change anything in it — you can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Just want to stop using the app?

Uninstalling PrankFX does not delete anything on our server. Use Settings → Delete account if that is what you want.
