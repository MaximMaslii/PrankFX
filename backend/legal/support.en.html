Frequently asked

How do FX credits work?

Every generation spends credits: 1 FX for a photo effect, 10 FX for a Snap clip. New accounts get a few free credits, and more can be bought in the app. If a generation fails, the credits go back to your balance automatically — usually within seconds. If they do not, write to us with the date and time and we will restore them.

I was charged but my credits did not arrive

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store, and occasionally a confirmation is delayed. Open Settings → Restore purchases first. If the balance is still wrong after a few minutes, send us the store receipt (the order number is enough) and we will sort it out.

I cannot sign in with Google

Check that your device has a network connection and that Google Play services are up to date, then try again. If the app shows a specific message, send it to us verbatim — it usually names the cause exactly.

My Snap clip failed

Snap clips are assembled on our server using an external AI model, and the model occasionally rejects a clip or times out. Your FX credits are returned automatically. Record again in better light, hold the phone steady, and keep the whole subject in frame. If it keeps failing, tell us which effect you used.

Can I use a photo of my friend?

Only with their consent. Photos of identifiable people without permission, and any sexual, violent or deceptive content, are not allowed — see section 6 of the Terms of Service.

A result looks wrong or offensive

The images and clips come from third-party AI models and are not curated by us in advance. Send us the effect name and a screenshot and we will investigate; we remove effects that repeatedly produce unacceptable output.

How do I report abuse or a content violation?

Email us with the word REPORT in the subject, describe what you saw and where, and attach evidence if you have it. We review reports of illegal content, harassment and images of minors as a priority and act within 48 hours.

How do I delete my account and my data?

In the app: Settings → Delete account. Full details, including what is deleted and how to request deletion by email, are on the Data deletion page.
