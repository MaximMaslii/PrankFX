This policy explains what PrankFX collects, why, who else sees it, and how you get rid of it. It covers the PrankFX mobile app (package com.prankfx.app) and the PrankFX backend service.

1. Who is responsible

PrankFX is operated by Maxim Maslii, an individual developer based in Ukraine ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described below.

Contact for any privacy question, request or complaint: maxim.maslii777@gmail.com. We answer within 5 business days and fulfil data-rights requests within 30 days.

2. What we collect

2.1 Account data

  • Email address — from your Google account when you use "Continue with Google", or typed by you when you register with a password.
  • Display name and profile picture URL — only when you sign in with Google, and only if your Google account shares them.
  • Password hash — only for email registration. We store a bcrypt hash; we never store or see your actual password.
  • Account metadata — an internal user id, sign-in method, creation date, your FX credit balance and subscription status.

2.2 Content you create

  • Photos you pick from your gallery or take with the camera in order to apply an effect.
  • Snap clips — a five-second video recording including its audio track, made inside the app.
  • Generated results — the images and clips our processing produces, plus the effect name and creation date.

2.3 Technical data

Our server keeps ordinary request logs: IP address, timestamp, the endpoint called, response status and error messages. They exist to keep the service running and to investigate abuse and outages. We keep them for up to 30 days.

2.4 What we do not do

PrankFX contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs and no third-party trackers. We do not build advertising profiles, we do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not use your photos or clips to train our own models.

3. Device permissions

The app asks for permissions only at the moment a feature needs them, and you can withdraw them at any time in your device settings:

  • Camera — to take a photo or record a Snap clip.
  • Microphone — to record the audio of a Snap clip. Audio is recorded only while a clip is being recorded.
  • Photo library — to let you pick a source photo and to save finished results back to your gallery.

The app does not access your contacts, location, calendar, call logs or SMS.

5. Who else processes your data

To produce an effect we have to send your content to the AI provider that generates it. We send only the file and the effect instruction — never your email, name or account id.

  • Google LLC — Gemini API. Photo effects are generated by Google's Gemini image models. We use the paid tier of the Gemini API, under which Google does not use submitted content to improve its products. See the Gemini API Terms.
  • Google LLC — Google Sign-In. Authentication. Google tells us your email, name and picture; we never receive your Google password. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Decart AI — "Lucy" video model. Snap clips are generated by Decart. For a recorded Snap only the 3.8-second segment that needs the effect leaves our server; for a Snap made from a photo, that photo (downscaled, no account details) is sent and deleted from our server once the clip is ready. See Decart's Privacy Policy and API Terms.
  • Features and Labels Inc. (fal.ai) — PixVerse v5.5 video model. Snap clips made from a photo are generated through fal.ai. The photo you choose (downscaled, with no account details attached) is sent for that one clip and deleted from our server as soon as the clip is ready. See fal's Privacy Policy and Terms.
  • Our hosting provider, which runs the server and database on our instructions.
  • Google Play / Apple App Store, if you buy FX credits. The store handles the payment; we never receive your card number or bank details — only the confirmation that a purchase succeeded.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, or to establish or defend legal claims.

6. International transfers

Google, Decart and fal.ai process data outside Ukraine and the EEA, including in the United States. Those transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the providers' own safeguards, as described in their policies linked above.

7. How long we keep things

  • Account data — until you delete your account.
  • Photo results in your history — until you delete the item or your account.
  • The raw video you record — deleted from the server immediately after the clip is assembled. Only the finished clip and its thumbnail are kept.
  • Finished Snap clips — until you delete the clip or your account.
  • Server logs — up to 30 days.
  • Purchase records — as long as tax and accounting law requires.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR and Ukraine's data protection law you may: access your data; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to processing; receive a copy in a portable format; and withdraw consent at any time. You can also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

The fastest route for deletion is inside the app: Settings → Delete account. This erases your account, your photo history and your Snap clips, including the files on our server. For anything else, email us at maxim.maslii777@gmail.com. Details: Data deletion.

9. Security

Traffic between the app and our server is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. Sessions use signed tokens that expire. Access to the production database is restricted to the developer. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach ever affects your data we will notify you and the competent authority as the law requires.

10. Children

PrankFX is not intended for children. You must be at least 13 years old to use it — and at least 16 if you are in the European Economic Area, unless your local law sets a lower age and your parent or guardian consents. Some effects (for example bruise effects) are marked as age-restricted and require an extra confirmation. If we learn that we hold data from a child below those ages, we delete it.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we update the date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we notify you in the app before the change takes effect.
